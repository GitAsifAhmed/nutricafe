How to play
  • Drag anywhere (or use ← → keys) to move your thali.
  • Catch healthy food for points. Keep catching to raise your multiplier.
  • Fill your plate with a protein, a grain and a vegetable or fruit for a Balanced plate bonus.
  • Junk food costs a heart and resets your multiplier. Lose all 3 and the round ends.
  • 💧 Water slows everything down for a few seconds. Every 3rd balanced plate gives back a heart.
