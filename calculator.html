Method

How the numbers
are worked out

01Per-100 g values

82 of 93 ingredients use the Food Composition Table for Bangladesh (FAO/INFS, Dhaka University), measured on local foods. The rest use USDA FoodData Central. Every ingredient shows its source.

02Units to grams

Spoons, cups and pieces are converted to grams - a tablespoon of oil is 13.8 g, a farm egg about 50 g edible, a level teaspoon of salt about 6 g.

03Edible portion

Bone-in chicken and fish are entered at purchase weight; their values already account for the bone you don't eat.

04Oil retention

The cooking method sets how much oil stays in the food. Curries keep it all; deep-frying keeps only what is absorbed.
